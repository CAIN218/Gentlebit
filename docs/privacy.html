Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how Gentlebit (the "Site", operated by the individual developer Yuga Tanaka) and the apps published on it handle information.

1. Information we collect

Contact form. If you write to us through the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you enter. We use them only to read and reply to your message, and we do not share them with third parties for any other purpose.

Analytics and cookies. The Site does not use analytics tools or advertising, and it does not set cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated first.

Apps. The apps on this Site work on your own device, and the developer does not collect personal information through them. Settings are stored in files on your device only. AudioSwitcher and Lumina connect to GitHub to check whether a newer version exists and, when you choose or enable automatic updates, to download it; GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address (see below). MeatCam uses your camera, and any photo you choose from your photo library, only to analyze the image on your device. It does not save or send the camera image or the photo, and has no network features.

2. Services we rely on

  • Formspree receives and delivers the contact form submissions (Formspree Privacy Policy).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Site, and GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit (GitHub Privacy Statement).

3. Data stored in your browser

The Site remembers your scroll position for the current browser tab only, so that your browser's Back button returns you to where you were. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the services listed above as needed to run the Site, or when required by law.

5. Why we use your information, and how long we keep it

We use the name, email address and message you send only to respond to your inquiry, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us. We delete a message once we have finished responding and a reasonable period has passed (about one year). Formspree keeps submissions according to its own policy.

6. Transfers outside your country

Formspree is based in the United States, and GitHub, which hosts the Site, also operates there. Contact form submissions and technical information such as IP addresses may therefore be processed outside Japan and outside your country of residence.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, delete or stop using the information you sent. Contact us through the contact form. If you live in the EU, the EEA or another region with data protection law, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date above. The Web fonts used on the Site are served from the Site itself.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form.

These tools are provided as-is by an individual developer, without warranty of any kind. The developer is not responsible for any data loss, system problems or other damage caused by their use. Use them at your own risk.
