Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how Yuga's Lab (the "Site", operated by the individual developer Yuga Tanaka) and the apps published on it handle information.

1. Information we collect

Contact form. If you write to us through the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you enter. We use them only to read and reply to your message, and we do not share them with third parties for any other purpose.

Analytics and cookies. The Site does not use analytics tools or advertising, and it does not set cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated first.

Apps. The apps on this Site are designed to work on your own device, and the developer does not collect personal information through them. Some apps contact GitHub to check whether a newer version is available.

2. Services we rely on

  • Formspree receives and delivers the contact form submissions (Formspree Privacy Policy).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Site, and GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Google Fonts serves the web fonts used on the Site, so your browser requests them from Google (Google Privacy Policy).

3. Data stored in your browser

The Site remembers your scroll position for the current browser tab only, so that the Back button returns you to where you were. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the services listed above as needed to run the Site, or when required by law.

5. Your choices

To ask us to correct or delete a message you sent, contact us through the contact form.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date above.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form.
